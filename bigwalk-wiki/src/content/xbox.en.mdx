## Crossplay Softens The Platform Gap

Because Big Walk supports **crossplay**, the lack of an Xbox version is less painful than it sounds for mixed friend groups. An Xbox-owning player who also has a PC can grab the Steam version and join sessions with friends on PS5 and Switch 2 seamlessly. The platform fragmentation that usually kills co-op game nights is exactly what crossplay solves here.

This works in every direction. A group of three PS5 players, two Steam players, and one Switch 2 player can all share one session — the only structural rule is who hosts, because the save file lives with the host's account. Pick a host, share the invitation, and the walk is on regardless of who owns what.

The one hard requirement remains: Big Walk is **co-op only**. Whichever platform you land on, you need at least one friend online (up to 12), and the host's save is the one everyone joins.

## How An Xbox Household Can Play Big Walk Tonight

The fastest route for most Xbox-only players is the one already sitting on a desk: a PC running Steam. The whole flow works like this. One person buys the game on Steam and hits **Host Game**, names a save file, and picks a group size — 2, 3, or 4+. Everyone else joins that session, from Steam, PS5, Mac, or Switch 2. Progress saves automatically, and the campaign lives in the host's save file, so the same person should host each session.

Two things make the experience work. First, **use the in-game proximity voice chat** rather than a separate call — House House strongly recommends it, because the muffled, distant audio is the core of the puzzles. Second, **play with friends, not strangers** — reviewers were unanimous that the game is built around people you know. With 2 to 12 players supported, even one friend is enough to start, though the game shines at four or more.
