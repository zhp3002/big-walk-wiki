## Crossplay On Steam

Big Walk supports **crossplay**, which is critical for a co-op-only game. A Steam player can join the same session as friends on PS5 and Nintendo Switch 2. You do not need your whole group on PC — platform mixing is fully supported. This removes the single biggest pain of PC multiplayer games (the "which store did you buy it on" problem) for your friend group.

Because the save file is tied to the session **host**, decide among your group who will host the ongoing save. That person's platform becomes the "home" for your campaign, though anyone can join from any supported platform. Progress saves automatically, and the group picks up from the host's last session.

## How Many Players On Steam

Big Walk scales from **2 to 12 players**. Two is described by House House as a focused co-op experience; twelve is, in their own words, "an absolute mess" (affectionately). When the host starts a session, they pick a group size mode — **2, 3, or 4+** — which slightly adjusts the puzzles so they remain solvable for your headcount.

The game is **co-op only**: there is no single-player mode on Steam or anywhere else. You need at least one friend online to play. So before buying on Steam, the real question is whether you have a crew — the game is only as good as the people you walk with.

## Proximity Voice Chat And Co-op Design

Big Walk's signature mechanic is **proximity-based voice chat** — voices fade over distance, muffle through walls, echo in tunnels, and crackle over walkie-talkies. The Steam version supports this fully. House House strongly recommends using the in-game voice chat rather than a separate Discord call, because muffled and distant audio is core to the puzzles and the experience.

The game is built around communication under constraint. Many puzzles separate players with soundproof walls or large distances, forcing you to invent your own signaling system with flashlights, laser pointers, and arm gestures. Reviewers repeatedly called this communication-as-puzzle design genuinely novel — it is the reason the game reviews so well.

## Starting Your First Session On Steam

Once you own the game, the flow on Steam is the same as every platform. Whoever is hosting hits **Host Game** on the main screen and creates or picks a save file — the save can be named anything, and the name is always editable. The host is then asked how many people are playing: **two, three, or four-plus**. You can switch between these sizes whenever you like, with no harm to the save file, which is handy when a regular cannot make a session.

Only the host needs to think about saving at all. Big Walk saves automatically — just exit, and progress continues next time. The one catch is that the save file is stored with the host, so the rest of the group cannot access it unless the host is online and running that session. Pick your host accordingly.
