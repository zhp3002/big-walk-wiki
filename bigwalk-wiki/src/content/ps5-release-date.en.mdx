Big Walk released on **PlayStation 5 on August 4, 2026** — the same date as every other platform, and the game is **out now**. What made the PS5 version special was not the date but the **region-tiered unlock**: PS5 players in the EU, UK, Australia, and New Zealand got in first, and PS5 players in the US unlocked about **five hours later**. On top of that, **PS Plus members could claim Big Walk for free at launch**, which made the PS5 version the best-value way to play.

## PS5 Release Date And Day-One Access

The PS5 release date was **August 4, 2026**, confirmed by listings on the regional PlayStation Stores. There was never a separate "PS5 delay" — House House shipped all four platforms (PC, Mac, PS5, Switch 2) on the same day. The only difference was the *hour* the game became playable, which was governed by your PSN region, not by Sony globally.

If you missed the launch rush, nothing is lost: the PS5 version is available on the PlayStation Store now, and the day-one mechanics below still explain how the release rolled out.

## PS5 Unlock Times: EU/AU First, US Later

The PS5 unlock was handled per-PSN-store, and this was the detail that caught people out. The **EU and UK stores** and the **Australia/New Zealand store** unlocked Big Walk first, at **9am BST / 10am CEST** — which was **6pm AEST / 8pm NZST** for the AU/NZ store. Roughly **five hours after that**, the **US store** unlocked at **6am PDT / 9am EDT**. So two PS5 owners — one in London, one in New York — could be playing five hours apart despite owning the same version.

Because the gap fell overnight for most of the Americas, the practical advice was to wait for your own region rather than chase early access. The difference was only five hours, and it arrived while most US players were asleep.

## The PS5 Unlock Schedule At A Glance

The regional PlayStation Store times for launch day:

- **PS5 (EU store):** 9am BST / 10am CEST — first wave
- **PS5 (AU/NZ store):** 6pm AEST / 8pm NZST — first wave, same moment as the EU window
- **PS5 (US store):** 6am PDT / 9am EDT — second wave, five hours later

The second wave was also when **Steam unlocked worldwide**, so from the US window onward, PS5 and PC players were starting together. The Switch 2 unlock time was never published for any region — that remains **to be confirmed**.

## Big Walk Was Free On PS Plus At Launch

This was the biggest reason to pick PS5 if you owned one: Big Walk was **free for PlayStation Plus members** during the launch window. You did not pay the full retail price — you claimed it as part of your membership. That made the PS5 the strongest value platform for the game, assuming you already subscribed to PS Plus.

If you were choosing between buying on Steam and playing on PS5, and you were already a PS Plus member, the PS5 route effectively cost you nothing extra at launch. Whether the free claim window is still open now is **to be confirmed** — check the PlayStation Store listing for Big Walk to see the current offer status, and confirm which PS Plus tier applies.

## Playing Big Walk Early On PS5

There was **no New Zealand clock trick** on PS5 — you could not manually set your console clock to NZST to unlock the game sooner, the way Xbox players sometimes exploit regional launches. The only legitimate early-access path was to **buy Big Walk from an earlier-rolling PSN store** (UK, EU, or AU/NZ) using an account set to that region.

For most players the five-hour overnight gap was not worth the hassle of a foreign PSN account. Waiting for your native region was the standard recommendation, and it was free if you were on PS Plus. Now that every store has unlocked, this is purely retrospective — no PS5 player anywhere is waiting on a region any more.

## Crossplay Means Your PS5 Friends Are Covered

Big Walk supports **crossplay**, so a PS5 player can join sessions with friends on PC, Mac, or Nintendo Switch 2. You never needed to all buy the same platform. This mattered for the release because even when parts of a group unlocked earlier or later, everyone could still meet up in-game once each person had access — the host starts a session and invites across platforms.

Just remember the game is **co-op only**: you cannot start a solo session. The PS5 player who owns the save (the host) is the one everyone else joins, and the save file is tied to that host's account. Progress saves automatically, and the group continues from wherever the host left off.

## What The PS5 Version Included On Day One

![Four characters carrying binoculars and a walkie-talkie on a coastal cliff overlooking the ocean](/images/topics/cliff-items.jpg)

The PS5 release was not a trimmed console port — it shipped with the full feature set that defines Big Walk everywhere:

- **Proximity voice chat** — voices fade across distance, echo down corridors, muffle through walls, and crackle through walkie-talkies.
- **Gestures and movement** — pointing, waving, and flailing to communicate when talking is not an option.
- **Tools and toys** — walkie-talkies, laser pointers, binoculars, megaphones, flares, whiteboards, cowbells, and a big golden head, among others.
- **2 to 12 players** with group-size settings of 2, 3, or 4+ that adjust puzzles to your headcount.
- **Crossplay** with PC, Mac, and Switch 2.
- **A hand-crafted ending** — the game is designed to be played start-to-finish with a group of friends.

The official feature set also lists **12 achievements** to work through, so completionist groups have a target beyond the final gate.
