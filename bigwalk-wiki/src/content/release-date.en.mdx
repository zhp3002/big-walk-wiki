## Which Platforms Got Big Walk On Release Date

Big Walk shipped on four platforms on August 4, 2026: **PC via Steam, Mac, PlayStation 5, and Nintendo Switch 2**. Importantly, it is a **Switch 2** title, not a game for the original Nintendo Switch — so players on the first-generation console were left out at launch. There is no Xbox version, and House House has not announced one. If you are shopping for the release date on any of those four platforms, you were covered on day one; if you were holding out for a fifth, there was nothing to buy.

## Release Times Differed By Region, Not Date

While the *date* was the same everywhere, the *hour* the game became playable depended on where you lived and which platform you used. PS5 players in the EU, UK, Australia, and New Zealand unlocked the game first. Roughly **five hours later**, PS5 players in the United States got access, and that is when PC and Mac players on Steam unlocked worldwide too.

This meant Steam players waited for the US-aligned global window, not the earlier EU one. The Nintendo Switch 2 unlock time was never officially published — that detail remains **to be confirmed**, though based on typical third-party releases it was expected to mirror the Steam window.

## The Full Unlock Schedule

The launch schedule below is drawn from the regional PlayStation Store listings and SteamDB, tracked around launch day:

- **PS5 (EU store):** 9am BST / 10am CEST
- **PS5 (AU/NZ store):** 6pm AEST / 8pm NZST
- **PS5 (US store):** 6am PDT / 9am EDT
- **Steam (PC and Mac, worldwide):** 6am PDT / 9am EDT / 2pm BST / 3pm CEST / 11pm AEST / 1am NZST on August 5

Two details are worth noticing. First, the EU and AU/NZ PS5 windows were the same moment expressed in different time zones — together they formed the "first" wave. Second, the Steam unlock was a single global instant, which is why New Zealand PC players technically started at 1am on August 5 local time even though the release date was August 4.

## How People Played Big Walk Early (Legitimately)

Because the PS5 unlock was tied to your PSN store region, players in the United States who wanted the earliest possible access could purchase Big Walk from the UK, EU, or Australia/New Zealand PSN store. The real region difference was about five hours, and it happened overnight for most US players, which is why most guides recommended simply waiting for your own region.

There was no "New Zealand trick" of the kind Xbox players sometimes use — on PS5 you could not fiddle the internal console clock to jump ahead. The cleanest early-access path was a PSN region purchase, and the cleanest free path was claiming the game through **PlayStation Plus** at launch. None of this matters any more, of course: every platform has been unlocked since launch day.

## What To Expect Now That Big Walk Is Out

Big Walk is a co-op-only open-world puzzle adventure, and crucially **you cannot play it alone** — you need at least one friend online, and the game scales up to 12 players. The session host picks a group size (2, 3, or 4+) at the start of each session, which slightly adjusts the puzzles to fit your headcount. The most important preparation is not about the game itself but about lining up your group.

The game supports **crossplay**, so your friends do not all need the same platform. A PC player, a PS5 player, and a Switch 2 player can all join the same session. Remember that the save file lives with the session host, so pick a reliable person to carry the ongoing campaign — everyone else joins them.

The island runs on a **day-night cycle**, with sun-soaked days and pitch-dark nights, and reviewers repeatedly found that the best moments happened when plans fell apart after dark. There are also **12 achievements** to complete across the adventure for groups who like a checklist, and the world is designed to be played start-to-finish with the same friends rather than sampled alone.
