## Big Walk On PS5: Release And Regions

Big Walk hit PS5 on **August 4, 2026**, the same date as PC, Mac, and Switch 2. The PS5 unlock was **region-tiered via PSN**: the EU and UK stores and the Australia/New Zealand store unlocked first at **9am BST / 10am CEST** (6pm AEST / 8pm NZST for AU/NZ), and the US store followed about **five hours later** at **6am PDT / 9am EDT**. Two PS5 owners in different regions could therefore start five hours apart on the same day.

For most players this gap fell overnight, so waiting for your native region was the standard advice. The date was the same everywhere — only the hour shifted, and now every store is unlocked.

## PS5 Crossplay With Other Platforms

Big Walk supports **crossplay**, so your PS5 copy is not locked to other PlayStation players. A PS5 player can join the same session as friends on **PC, Mac, and Nintendo Switch 2**. This matters because Big Walk is co-op only — you cannot play alone — so being able to mix platforms means your friend group is never split by who owns what console.

The session **host** owns the save file, so decide within your group who will host the ongoing campaign. Anyone, on any supported platform, can join that host's sessions. Saving is automatic, and there is no manual save to worry about — the host just exits and progress continues next time.

## How Many Players On PS5

On PS5 (as on every platform) Big Walk supports **2 to 12 players**. The host picks a group-size mode — **2, 3, or 4+** — at the start of each session, and the game slightly adjusts puzzles to stay solvable for that headcount. House House calls 2 players a focused co-op experience and 12 players "an absolute mess", in the most affectionate sense.

You need at least one friend online to play. There is no solo mode on PS5 or anywhere else — this is a deliberate design choice by House House, and reviewers consistently noted that the game is only as good as your group.

## Proximity Voice Chat On PS5

Big Walk's defining mechanic is **proximity-based voice chat**: voices fade across distance, muffle through walls, echo in tunnels, and crackle over in-game walkie-talkies. The PS5 version supports this fully, and House House strongly recommends using the in-game voice chat instead of a party chat or Discord call — the constrained audio is the point of the puzzles.

Many puzzles separate players with soundproof walls or large distances, forcing you to invent signaling systems using flashlights, laser pointers, and gestures. This communication-under-pressure design is what makes Big Walk so distinctive. A headset is effectively required equipment: you will be talking, whispering, and honking at each other for hours.
