If you are standing under a sign that reads **4166** and **1899** and hunting around for a keypad, you can stop. **4166 and 1899 are not a combination — they are coordinates, latitude and longitude, and they point to the location of a hidden button on the island.** There is nothing to type the numbers into. The actual puzzle is working out where those coordinates land, walking there, and holding down the button you find so a teammate can collect the prize.

You run into this puzzle early, in a structure near the beach: an oblong prize sits sealed inside a frosted glass container, and the sign with the two four-digit numbers hangs on a hook above it. One more thing to know before you grind on it: **when you first find this puzzle, most likely just after the tutorial puzzles, you cannot solve it yet.** The items that make those numbers mean something come later in the game. Here is the full breakdown.

## What Do 4166 and 1899 Mean?

The two numbers are **grid coordinates for the island** — one latitude, one longitude — and together they mark the spot where the hidden button is buried. The button is what actually opens the frosted glass container back at the puzzle building near the beach.

If you have already seen a prize in a similar container earlier in the game, you know the routine: those containers only open while **one of your companions holds down a button somewhere else**. This puzzle is the same mechanic, except this time the game does not show you where the button is. Instead of a marked location, you get two bare four-digit numbers on a sign — and it is up to your group to translate them into a place on the island.

That is why the numbers look like a code at first. Four digits, two of them, hanging on a hook — of course your group tries entering them somewhere. There is no lock, no dial and no door that accepts them. They only start to make sense once your group owns an item that can read the island's grid.

## What You Need Before Solving the Puzzle

The most important thing to understand: **if you have just finished the tutorial puzzles, this one is simply not solvable yet — and that is by design.** PC Gamer's advice is to leave it for now and come back later, after your group has solved around five other puzzles and completed the [red tower](/en/topics/red-tower-puzzle). You are not missing a hidden keypad or a missed clue. You are missing gear.

There are **two different items that can crack the number puzzle**, and either one is enough on its own:

- **The map** — a foldable map (plus a compass) that shows the island's numbered grid, unlocked through red tower progress.
- **The GPS** — a handheld device with a screen that displays your **current coordinates** wherever you stand, found near the green tower. The full rundown of both items — and every other gadget — is in our [tools and items guide](/en/topics/tools).

One of those two items turns 4166 1899 from nonsense into an address. Which one you go for is mostly a matter of how far your group has already progressed, so both routes are laid out below.

## How to Solve the 4166 1899 Puzzle

**Step 1: Leave it and make progress first.** If the numbers mean nothing to your group yet, walk away — seriously. Come back once you have solved about five other puzzles and completed the red tower. That progress unlocks the navigation items the puzzle quietly assumes you have.

**Step 2: Get the map or the GPS.** Completing the red tower — which means placing five collectibles on its roof — opens the map room right next to the starting hub, which contains a large floor map plus a portable foldable map and compass. Alternatively, later in the game you can pick up the GPS from a red frame just outside the green tower. Either tool is enough.

**Step 3: Find the grid square the numbers point to.** The coordinates resolve to a search area **east and south of the starter hub** (exact map-reading and GPS walkthroughs are in the next two sections). In that square you are hunting for a **small button carefully camouflaged behind a rock** — scan the rocks closely, because it genuinely blends in.

**Step 4: Split your group and grab the prize.** One player stays at the button and **holds it down**, while another player goes back to the puzzle building near the beach and **grabs the prize the moment the glass container opens**. The holder cannot see the container and the grabber cannot see the button, so bring **walkie-talkies** — the distance between the two spots is too far for proximity voice chat.

## How to Use the Map Coordinates

The map route is the quicker of the two once the red tower is done. Five collectibles placed on the red tower's roof open the **map room right next to the starting hub**. Inside is a large map laid out on the floor for the whole group to gather around, plus a **portable foldable map and a compass** you can carry with you into the field.

To read the coordinates on the foldable map, match the numbers to the grid lines:

- **41** is your longitude — follow the numbered lines running along the **sides** of the map until you reach 41.
- **18** is your latitude — follow the numbered lines along the **top and bottom** of the map to 18.
- Where those lines cross is the **grid square** you need to search, east and south of the starter hub.

The last two digits of each number fine-tune your search within that square. The **66** in 4166 means the spot sits about **two-thirds of the way south from the top of the grid square**, and the **99** in 1899 means it is about **as far east as the square allows, right along the eastern gridline**. Walk to that corner of the square and start checking behind rocks.

## How to Solve the Puzzle With the GPS

The GPS route takes longer to reach but is arguably more fun. The device sits in a **red frame just outside the green tower** — the same general area where the hidden button eventually is — next to another sign printed with more numbers. By the time your group reaches the green tower, the number puzzle's secret is already out.

The GPS shows the **coordinates of wherever its holder is standing**. You can hold it up to show friends, or **stick it onto someone's backpack** so it travels hands-free. That backpack trick is the intended play:

1. One player wears the GPS on their back.
2. A second player watches the screen and **directs them which way to walk**.
3. As the wearer moves across the island, the readout changes — **watch whether the numbers drift toward or away from 4166 1899** to tell if you are getting closer or further.
4. Zero in on the target numbers, then start searching the rocks.

Players on the r/BigWalk subreddit describe solving the puzzle exactly this way — walking while watching the GPS numbers converge on the target coordinates. Treat the community method as player experience rather than official doctrine, but it lines up with how the item demonstrably works.

## What to Do When You Reach the Coordinates

You have arrived when you spot the **small button camouflaged behind a rock**. It is deliberately easy to walk past, so sweep the target square slowly and check the shaded sides of rocks rather than open ground.

The finale is pure two-player teamwork, and it cannot be done solo: **one player holds the button down while another player travels back to the puzzle structure near the beach and takes the prize out of the now-open glass container.** The moment the holder lets go, the container closes — so agree on the plan before anyone presses anything. Because the two spots are far apart, bring **walkie-talkies** so the holder and the grabber can coordinate the timing.

## FAQ

### What do 4166 and 1899 mean in Big Walk?

They are **coordinates — latitude and longitude** — that mark the location of a hidden button on the island. They are not a combination code, and there is no keypad in the game that accepts them.

### Can you solve the 4166 1899 puzzle immediately?

No. If you have just finished the tutorial puzzles, you lack the items needed to read the coordinates. The recommended move is to leave it, solve around five other puzzles, complete the red tower, and return with the map or the GPS.

### Where do you get the map in Big Walk?

Completing the **[red tower](/en/topics/red-tower-puzzle)** — placing five collectibles on its roof — opens the **map room next to the starting hub**, which holds a large floor map plus a portable foldable map and compass.

### Where do you find the GPS in Big Walk?

Just outside the **green tower**, sitting in a red frame alongside another sign with numbers on it. The green tower area is also roughly where the hidden button is.

### Can you solve the puzzle with the GPS?

Yes. The GPS displays your live coordinates, so one player can wear it on their backpack while another reads the screen and directs them — moving until the numbers match 4166 1899.

### Do you need another player to complete the 4166 1899 puzzle?

Yes. The final step takes two people at once: one to **hold down the hidden button** and another to **grab the prize from the opened container** at the puzzle building. That fits the game overall — Big Walk is online co-op for 2-12 players with no solo mode.

## Summary

4166 and 1899 are **coordinates, not a code** — they point to a camouflaged button east and south of the starter hub. Read 41/18 as grid lines, use 66 and 99 to fine-tune to the south-eastern edge of the square, then split up: one player holds the button while another grabs the prize. Bring walkie-talkies.
