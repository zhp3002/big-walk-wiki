**Big Walk's community lives on the Steam Community forums, the player-run Big Walk Reddit community, and developer House House's own channels — and there is no official Big Walk Discord server**, a point worth stating plainly because unofficial Discords routinely get mistaken for official ones. Since the game's August 4, 2026 launch, these spaces have filled with players looking for co-op groups, sharing clips, and helping each other through puzzles. Because Big Walk is a co-op-only game with no solo mode, the community matters more than for most titles: you need people to play with, and the right group defines your experience. Here is where the Big Walk community lives, how to find a group, and what to expect now that launch has passed.

## Where The Big Walk Community Lives

The main places to engage with other Big Walk players are:

- **Steam Community Discussions** — the PC player base's forum for troubleshooting, finding groups, and trading tips. Useful if you are playing on Steam.
- **The Big Walk Reddit community** — a player-run community, not an official one, where clips, stories, and group-finding posts circulate. Player-run Discord servers are shared there too, but they are not official House House spaces.
- **House House's official channels** — the developer's website, the official FAQ, and House House's official YouTube channel, which carry announcements and the official videos rather than player discussion.
- **Official store pages** — Big Walk's Steam and PlayStation pages carry official information alongside player reviews.

If you are deciding where to start, the Steam forums and the Reddit community are the most active entry points, since there is no official Discord to default to.

## There Is No Official Big Walk Discord

To be explicit: **House House has not provided an official Big Walk Discord server**, and no official Discord for the studio or the game has been identified. If you see a Discord advertised as the "official Big Walk Discord," treat that claim with caution — the Discords that do exist are player-run spaces organized through Reddit and elsewhere.

That does not make those spaces useless; player-run communities are often where the most active group-finding happens. It just means nobody in them speaks for House House, and moderation, events, and rules are entirely volunteer-run. Verify anything presented as an official announcement against House House's own channels before acting on it. If an official Discord appears later, it would be announced through those channels — until then, assume any server you find is unofficial.

## Why Community Matters More For Big Walk

For most games, the community is a nice-to-have. For Big Walk it is **essential**, because the game cannot be played solo. Every session requires a group of 2 to 12 players, and the quality of your experience is tied directly to the people you play with. Reviewers are unanimous that Big Walk is at its best with a consistent group of friends over several sessions — but not everyone has a ready-made crew.

This is where the community fills the gap, and reviewers predicted exactly that: a community forming around connecting strangers to play Big Walk together, with plenty of people having a good time that way. If your real-life friends are not available or not interested, the Steam forums and Reddit are where you find committed strangers who also need a group. Treat a community-found group like a real co-op commitment: show up, communicate, and play regularly.

The advice that follows from reviewers is blunt: if you can possibly play with people you know and love dearly, get them to make time instead — one night a week for about a month covers the campaign. Big Walk is a game about relationships as much as puzzles, and the emotional payoff lands harder with friends. Community-found groups absolutely work, but they take more deliberate effort to hold together, because the game's design (and its host-tied save) assumes a stable crew.

## Finding A Co-op Group

Big Walk's roughly 20-hour, start-to-finish campaign is best played with a **consistent group across multiple sessions**, so finding the right people matters. Tips for finding a group through the community:

- **Post your platform, region, and availability** when looking for players. Crossplay means PC, PS5, and Switch 2 players can all play together, so platform mixing is fine.
- **Look for a group planning a fresh start** if you want the full start-to-finish experience, since joining mid-campaign on someone else's save can spoil puzzles.
- **Commit to a schedule** — because the save is tied to the host, a group that meets weekly works far better than ad-hoc drop-ins.
- **Use the in-game proximity voice chat** once playing — reviewers are emphatic that running a separate voice call underneath the game takes away a shocking amount of Big Walk's impact. Save the external call for organizing and use the game's own audio during play.

## What The Community Looks Like After Launch

Big Walk launched **August 4, 2026** to strong critical coverage from IGN, Kotaku, GameSpot, and Polygon, with several outlets calling it one of the best games of 2026. That reception drove a **large launch-window influx of players eager to form groups**, and the first weeks after release have been the easiest time to find partners, compare puzzle stories, and trade clips.

Because Big Walk is a finite co-op campaign rather than a live-service game, expect the community to keep evolving: a big launch peak of group-forming and clip-sharing, settling into a core of players running new campaigns and helping newcomers. The community will not look like a live-service game's persistent player base — and that is healthy for what Big Walk is. Each group's experience is self-contained, so the "is the game dead" question barely applies.

## Sharing The Best Moments

![Characters gathered around a glowing campsite on a dark hillside at night](/images/topics/night-camp.jpg)

A huge part of Big Walk's community culture is **sharing clips and stories**. The game generates emergent, comedic, and sometimes emotional moments from communication breakdowns — friends found by flare light in the dark, walkie-talkie singalongs, groups naming the island's mysterious objects in their own private vocabulary. Community spaces are full of players trading those stories, and reviewers describe Big Walk as a game that produces moments you keep messaging your friends about for years.

Recording your sessions and posting the highlights is genuinely part of the experience, not just promotion. It also serves the next wave of players: your clip of a puzzle solved through arm-waving alone might be exactly what convinces a hesitant group to buy in. Post your own, enjoy everyone else's, and mark spoilers considerately — the ending especially is worth protecting.

## Summary

Big Walk's community centers on **the Steam Community forums, the player-run Reddit community, and House House's official channels — with no official Discord existing**. Player-run Discords circulate on Reddit but should not be mistaken for official spaces. Community matters more for Big Walk than most games because it is co-op-only with no solo mode; find a committed group by posting your platform and availability, play on a consistent schedule, and use in-game proximity voice chat during sessions. Expect a large, active launch-window community settling into a long tail as groups finish, with clip-sharing as a core part of the culture.
