The **Red Tower is the tower you want done early** — not because the game forces you, but because of what it hands your group afterwards: the **Map Room**, a foldable **map** and a **compass**. Big Walk keeps its towers open in almost any order, but the Red Tower sits right next to where you begin, its five puzzles are early-game friendly, and its reward is the navigation kit that makes every later exploration — including the [4166 1899 coordinates puzzle](/en/topics/4166-1899-puzzle) — dramatically easier. Here is the full walkthrough: where the tower is, the five puzzles that feed it, and exactly how its key turns into the map.

A quick orientation for the items involved. Each of the five nearby puzzles pays out a **puzzle reward** — a small red collectible your group carries back to the tower. The game never officially names these; PC Gamer's writer calls them **"nubbins"**, other players say red beans or gourds. Deposit all five at the top of the Red Tower and the tower releases its key, and that key is what physically unlocks the Map Room.

## What Does the Red Tower Unlock?

The Red Tower's entire payout chain looks like this:

- **The Map Room** — a room containing a full-scale **diorama of the whole island**, with everything your group has already completed marked by a flag.
- **A foldable map and a compass** — sitting on a bench in the Map Room, both carryable, so you can navigate in the field instead of running back to the diorama.
- **The Big View achievement/trophy** — popping the Map Room door open completes it (per GameRant's walkthrough).
- **Coordinate literacy** — once you hold the map, the island's numbered grid becomes readable, which is exactly the skill the [4166 1899 number puzzle](/en/topics/4166-1899-puzzle) tests.

That last point is the practical link: PC Gamer's solution to the 4166 1899 puzzle explicitly routes players through the Red Tower first, because the map it grants is one of only two items that can decode those coordinates (the GPS near the green tower is the other). Do the Red Tower and the infamous number puzzle stops being a wall.

## How Many Red Tower Puzzles Do You Need to Solve?

**Five.** From the top of the Red Tower you can look toward the shoreline and literally see all five puzzle locations at once — the game clusters them deliberately. Each one pays out one puzzle reward, and the terminal at the top of the tower wants all five before it gives up its key.

GameRant names the five as:

1. **The Yellow Maze**
2. **The Green Button Room**
3. **The Train Station Timer Cannon**
4. **The Basketball Court**
5. **The Mountain Overlook Gesture puzzle**

A community guides roundup on the r/BigWalk subreddit covers the same block under "How to Unlock the Map Room (Red Tower puzzles)" and likewise counts five nearby puzzles. Reddit could not be re-fetched directly for this page, so treat that as a community guide's framing rather than an independently re-verified count — PC Gamer and GameRant, which were checked in full, both agree on five.

## How to Complete the Red Tower Puzzles

The step-by-step solutions below follow **GameRant's two-player walkthrough** — published August 4, 2026. The ideas hold for bigger groups, but the details can shift slightly with more players.

### Puzzle 1: The Yellow Maze

- **Where to find it:** The yellow maze structure, one of the five visible from the tower's top toward the shoreline.
- **What you need:** Two players, one wind-up timer, one green pot inside.
- **What to do:** Split up at the entrance — one player takes the **left path**, the other the **right**. The left-path player picks up the **wind-up timer** and hurries to the nearest **hole** connecting the two paths, passing the timer through to the right-path player, who rushes it to the **green pot** and drops it in before it winds down.
- **Useful tip:** Agree on which hole to use before you start — fumbling the handover while the timer ticks is the classic failure here.

### Puzzle 2: The Green Button Room

- **Where to find it:** The green button-room structure among the five shoreline locations.
- **What you need:** One player inside the closed room, one outside; walkie-talkies strongly recommended.
- **What to do:** One player enters the room and **closes the door**. The console **inside** the room lights up a button; the player inside calls out **exactly which button** to the player outside, who presses the matching button on the outside console. Repeat for each prompt.
- **Common mistake:** Vague callouts. The player outside can only see what you tell them — name the button precisely, and use a walkie-talkie so the door's walls don't eat your voice.

### Puzzle 3: The Train Station Timer Cannon

- **Where to find it:** The train station — yes, the same train the wider game eventually unlocks.
- **What you need:** Two players, a cannon, a wind-up timer, another green pot.
- **What to do:** One player stays on the **yellow platform** and fires the **cannon**, which launches the wind-up timer a great distance. The other player chases the timer down **before it ticks to zero**, climbs the **rock opposite the yellow platform**, and **throws the timer back over**, so the first player can slot it into the green pot in time.
- **Useful tip:** Watch where the cannon is aiming before it fires and start moving early — the return throw is easier if you're already on the rock.

### Puzzle 4: The Basketball Court

- **Where to find it:** The basketball court among the five locations.
- **What you need:** Any number of players; a light-up ball.
- **What to do:** Grab the **light-up bulb/ball** from the ground and **throw it through the basket**. Keep scoring until **all the lights turn green** — then the puzzle reward is released.
- **Common mistake:** Overthinking it. Of the five, this is the relaxed one.

### Puzzle 5: The Mountain Overlook Gesture Puzzle

- **Where to find it:** The mountain overlook — bring binoculars or a spyglass if you have them.
- **What you need:** Two players, telescopes at the overlook, gesture cards, five distant platforms; walkie-talkies essential.
- **What to do:** One player stays at the **overlook** and studies the **five distant platforms through the telescopes**. The other player travels to each platform, receives the **correct gesture** by callout, and builds it from **button combinations — for example left arm up, right arm up, crouch** — placing the matching gesture card into the rack.
- **Useful tip:** GameRant flags this as the slowest of the five. Binoculars help the traveler confirm they're on the right platform before committing to a gesture.

## How to Complete the Red Tower

With all five puzzle rewards in hand, the tower itself wraps up fast:

1. **Climb to the top** of the Red Tower and find the terminal with the key — the same kind of setup as the starting area.
2. **Insert all five puzzle rewards** into the terminal. Per PC Gamer this is the "five nubbins on the red tower" step; GameRant describes inserting the five items at the top terminal — the same deposit, worded differently.
3. **Take the released key** and head downstairs.

The key is not finished yet, though — it needs shaping first (next section).

## How to Unlock the Map Room

The key-to-map sequence, per GameRant's walkthrough:

1. **Start filing the key.** Directly in front of the Red Tower entrance is a small slot — insert the key to begin **shaving/filing** it.
2. **Visit the remaining filers on the way down.** The filing contraptions are lined up along the pathway leading back down toward the gym area — insert the key **four more times**, once at each contraption.
3. **Use the finished key.** The properly shaped key opens the **terminal at the top of the blue tunnel with red stairs**.
4. **Walk into the Map Room.** The tunnel door unlocks, and stepping inside the Map Room completes the **Big View** achievement/trophy.

The room's location is described two compatible ways in the coverage: GameRant places it behind the blue-tunnel-with-red-stairs door, while PC Gamer simply says the map room opens up **right next to the starting hub** — the tunnel is the starting-area-side door in question.

## What You Get From the Map Room

- **The island diorama** — a full-scale model of the entire map on the room's floor, with **flags marking everything your group has already completed**. It doubles as a planning table: gather the group around it and pick the next monument.
- **The foldable map** — on a bench in the room; pick it up and carry it. It shows the island's **numbered coordinate grid**, the same grid the 4166 1899 puzzle's numbers refer to.
- **The compass** — on the same bench, also carryable, for orienting the map in the field.

Why this matters: Big Walk deliberately ships with **no map screen and no quest markers**, so until you own these items your group navigates by landmark and memory. The map and compass replace guesswork with an actual grid reference — which is precisely why the 4166 1899 coordinates puzzle is unsolvable before this point and routine after it.

## Why You Should Complete the Red Tower Early

To be clear about what is and isn't a rule: **the game does not require the Red Tower first.** Guides agree the towers can be done in almost any order. This is practical advice, not a gate:

- The Red Tower is **next to the starting area** — the path uphill begins directly opposite the gym you pass through early on, and the game nudges you toward it right after the red bridge.
- Its five puzzles are **early-game difficulty**, solved with basic tools you already have.
- Its reward — the map, compass and diorama — **compounds over the whole campaign**, because every later puzzle hunt benefits from actually knowing where you are.
- The alternative route to coordinate literacy, the **GPS near the green tower**, comes later in progression than the map does.

PC Gamer's 4166 1899 walkthrough makes the same point from the puzzle side: groups that hit the number puzzle before doing "five other puzzles and the red tower" simply cannot solve it yet. Red Tower first, and that wall never appears.

## FAQ

### Where is the Red Tower in Big Walk?

Directly **opposite the gym** you pass through after the starting area — take the path uphill and you will reach it. It is the tower **shaped like an upside-down cone**, and the game nudges you toward it right after you cross the red bridge.

### How many puzzles are needed for the Red Tower in Big Walk?

**Five nearby puzzles.** From the top of the tower you can see all five locations toward the shoreline: the Yellow Maze, Green Button Room, Train Station Timer Cannon, Basketball Court, and Mountain Overlook Gesture puzzle.

### What does the Red Tower unlock?

The **Map Room** (a full island diorama with completed-content flags), a **foldable map**, a **compass**, and the **Big View** achievement/trophy for opening the room.

### How do you unlock the Map Room in Big Walk?

Deposit the Red Tower's **five puzzle rewards** at its top terminal, take the key, file it at the five shaving contraptions between the tower entrance and the gym area, then use the finished key on the terminal at the top of the **blue tunnel with red stairs**.

### How do you get the map in Big Walk?

Complete the Red Tower — five nearby puzzles, five puzzle rewards deposited — and unlock its Map Room. The **foldable map** waits on a bench inside, next to the compass.

### Where do you get the compass in Big Walk?

On the **same bench as the foldable map**, inside the Map Room the Red Tower opens.

### Should you complete the Red Tower first?

The game allows any tower order, so no — but it is **strongly recommended early**. It is next to the starting area, its puzzles are gentle, and its map-and-compass reward helps every exploration that follows.

### What are the five red items for?

Each is a **puzzle reward** from one of the five Red Tower puzzles. The tower's top terminal takes all five and releases the key that leads to the Map Room.

### Do you need the map for the 4166 1899 puzzle?

Not strictly — the **GPS** near the green tower also solves it — but the map is the earlier, easier route, and PC Gamer's solution treats Red Tower completion as the natural prerequisite.
