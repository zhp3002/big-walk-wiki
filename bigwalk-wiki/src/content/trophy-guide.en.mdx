## What Big Walk's 12 Achievements Likely Involve

- **Exploration and discovery** — given the open-world island and the celebration of getting lost, there may be achievements tied to discovery rather than pure completion.

Four towers, a campaign, a post-game, and a handful of puzzle or exploration milestones would roughly account for a list of 12. But that is arithmetic on plausible categories, **not confirmed trophy names** — wait for the official list before planning a hunt.

## Co-op Trophy Considerations

One thing worth flagging for Big Walk specifically: because the **save is tied to the host**, trophy progression may be bound to the host's save rather than each individual player's. This is common in co-op games and could matter a lot for a group planning a shared trophy hunt — for example, whether everyone in the session earns an unlock at the same moment, or whether guests who miss a session fall behind and need it re-triggered.

That behavior is **to be confirmed** once players have tested it across sessions. If you are organizing your group with achievements in mind, the safest approach is to keep the same crew together for the whole campaign, so any host-tied behavior never gets a chance to bite.

Also remember that Big Walk is **co-op only with no solo play** — it supports 2 to 12 players with session formats for two, three, and four-plus. Every one of the 12 achievements is therefore earned in a group context. There is no "complete this alone" achievement path, and no way to grind the list out solo.

## Planning Your Trophy Run

If you intend to earn all 12, plan the run before you start rather than after the credits. The campaign is roughly 20 hours with the group together, the save lives with the host, and there is no known way to return solo later to mop up — so anything your group does not trigger together may mean reassembling the same friends to trigger it. Until the trackers confirm how unlocks actually behave, structure your run so the question never comes up.

Practical steps worth taking:

- **Pick the host deliberately** — the steadiest scheduler in the group, since the save cannot be opened without them online.
- **Keep one roster** — a stable group means every unlock happens with everyone present, sidestepping attendance-based quirks before they are understood.
- **Agree on a cadence** — one night a week for about a month matches the reviewer pace and keeps the campaign from stalling.
- **Do not rush the ending** — you need almost every puzzle to reach the credits anyway, so a completionist route and a normal route are nearly the same trip.
