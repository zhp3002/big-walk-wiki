Big Walk has **13 trophies on PlayStation 5 — one platinum, ten gold and two silver** — and the same 13 achievements on Steam and Nintendo Switch 2. Almost every one is a simple exploration or story milestone (cross the drawbridge, ride the chairlift, visit the map), so this is a relaxed, unmissable platinum: if your group finishes the campaign together, you will unlock nearly the whole list on the way. Here is the full list with unlock conditions, how long the platinum takes, and the one co-op quirk worth knowing before you start.

## Trophy Walkthrough Notes

- **Big Walk (cross the drawbridge)** — pops minutes into the game, when your group lowers and crosses the drawbridge at the end of the tutorial area. Unlock rate is the highest of the set.
- **Big View (visit the map)** — step inside the Map Room unlocked by the [Red Tower chain](/en/topics/red-tower-puzzle). Only about a quarter of players have it, which tells you how many groups stop before their first tower.
- **Big Sit (ride the chairlift)** / **Big Ride (ride the train)** — the travel modes keys unlock later in the game; simply take one ride on each.
- **Big Tunnel / Big Wall** — pass through the named large structures as the campaign opens them up.
- **Big Pack (wear a backpack)** — grab a backpack from one of the round blue buildings and have a friend strap it onto you ([where to find backpacks](/en/topics/tools)).
- **Big Dress-Ups** — collect and wear 10 cosmetic dress-up items; the biggest "extra exploring" item on the list.
- **Big Chomp / Big Gnash** — eat every food item in the game, and (for Big Gnash) eat all of it in one session/gathering. Food is scattered around campsites and benches.
- **Big Scoop (read the news)** / **Big Group (complete a jigsaw)** — interact with the newspaper stand and finish the group jigsaw puzzle.
- **Big Trophy (platinum)** — unlocks automatically once the other 12 are yours.

## The Co-op Quirk: Trophies And The Host's Save

Big Walk's save lives with the **host**, so plan the platinum around one steady host and one roster. The safest structure: the same group, hosted by the same person, start to credits — every unlock then happens with everyone present, and nobody has to re-trigger anything later. Since there is no solo mode (the game is [co-op only, 2–12 players](/en/topics/solo)), there is no way to mop the list up alone afterwards.

For the collectible-style entries (dress-ups, food) it is worth having the group deliberately sweep areas together near the end of the campaign rather than hoping you passed everything.

## How Long Does The Platinum Take?

Roughly one campaign: reviewers spent about **20 hours** reaching a substantially complete state with puzzles still left, and the platinum needs the travel modes, the jigsaw, the food sweep and the dress-ups on top — budget **around 20–25 hours with your group**. Difficulty is low: nothing in the list demands skill or speedruns, only that your group keeps walking to the credits. ## FAQ

### How many trophies are in Big Walk?

**13 on PS5** (1 platinum, 10 gold, 2 silver), with the same 13 achievements on Steam and Switch 2.

### Does Big Walk have a platinum trophy?

Yes — **Big Trophy**, awarded for unlocking the other 12. It is an easy platinum by trophy-hunt standards: unmissable milestones, no difficulty or skill requirements.

### What is the rarest Big Walk trophy?

The platinum itself, followed by **Big View** (visit the map) at roughly a quarter of players — most groups that buy the game stop before finishing their first tower, not because the trophies are hard.

### Can you get Big Walk trophies playing solo?

No. Big Walk has no solo mode; every trophy is earned in a 2–12 player co-op session. Keep one host and one roster for the campaign.

### Are any Big Walk trophies missable?

No hard missables are known — the entries are one-time actions (ride, visit, eat, wear) rather than timed or route-dependent unlocks. Big Gnash (eat all the food in one go) is the one to deliberately plan rather than leave to chance.

### How long is the Big Walk platinum?

About 20–25 hours with a group — essentially one full playthrough plus a cleanup sweep for dress-ups and food.
