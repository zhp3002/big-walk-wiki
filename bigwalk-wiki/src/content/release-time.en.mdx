Big Walk did not unlock at the same hour everywhere. The game launched on **August 4, 2026**, and the exact release time depended on your **platform and region**. PS5 players in the EU, UK, Australia, and New Zealand got in first; about **five hours later**, PS5 players in the US plus all PC and Mac players on Steam unlocked worldwide. If you are hunting the precise Big Walk release time, the full schedule is below — and now that the game is out, every platform is unlocked.

## Every Confirmed Big Walk Release Time

The launch schedule was pieced together from the regional PlayStation Stores and SteamDB, and it breaks into two waves:

- **PS5 (EU store):** 9am BST / 10am CEST
- **PS5 (AU/NZ store):** 6pm AEST / 8pm NZST
- **PS5 (US store):** 6am PDT / 9am EDT
- **Steam (PC and Mac, worldwide):** 6am PDT / 9am EDT / 2pm BST / 3pm CEST / 11pm AEST / 1am NZST on August 5

In plain terms: Big Walk released first on PS5 in the UK/EU and Australia/New Zealand, then **five hours later** it launched on PS5 in the US alongside a simultaneous worldwide release on PC via Steam.

## PS5 Release Times By Region

On PlayStation 5, the Big Walk unlock was tied to your PSN store region. Players on the **EU and UK PSN stores**, along with **Australia and New Zealand**, unlocked the game first — that was 9am BST in the UK, 10am CEST in mainland Europe, 6pm AEST in Australia, and 8pm NZST in New Zealand, all the same moment. Five hours later, players on the **US PSN store** got access at 6am PDT / 9am EDT.

This regional stagger was the single biggest source of release-time confusion, because two PS5 owners in different regions could be playing five hours apart on the same calendar day. The five-hour gap happened overnight for most US players, which is why most guides recommended simply waiting for your own region rather than chasing early access.

## PC And Mac (Steam) Release Time

For everyone on **Steam** — whether PC or Mac — Big Walk unlocked in the **same global window as the US PS5 release**, roughly five hours after the EU/AU/NZ PS5 launch. Steam did not region-stagger the way PSN did, so PC and Mac players worldwide all started together at one shared moment.

That single global instant landed at very different local hours: a comfortable 6am on the US west coast, 9am on the US east coast, 2pm in the UK, and 3pm in mainland Europe — but 11pm AEST in Australia and **1am NZST on August 5** in New Zealand. So for some Steam players, "launch day" technically began on the next calendar date. The August 4 date was the anchor, not a promise of an evening start in your time zone.

## Nintendo Switch 2 Release Time

The **Nintendo Switch 2** unlock time was **never officially published** — that detail remains **to be confirmed**. Based on how third-party releases typically behaved on Nintendo's eShop, it was expected to mirror the Steam global window, but treat that as a reasonable expectation rather than a confirmed fact. Switch 2 players who picked the game up on day one simply found it available on the eShop when their region opened; the eShop listing was the authoritative source for the exact local hour.

What *is* confirmed for Switch 2 is the date: **August 4, 2026**, the same day as every other platform. So while we cannot tell you the hour the eShop flipped the switch, we can tell you it happened within the same launch day as the rest of the world — there was no separate Switch 2 release date or delay.

## Could You Play Big Walk Early?

On PS5, the only legitimate way to play early from a later-rolling region was to **purchase the game from an earlier PSN store** (UK, EU, or AU/NZ). That bought you roughly five hours of head-start. There was **no New Zealand clock trick** on PS5 like the one Xbox players sometimes use — you could not manually shift your console clock to jump the queue.

For most players the gap was small enough, and timed awkwardly enough (overnight), that waiting for your native region was the sensible call. If you were a **PS Plus member**, you could claim the game free at launch regardless of region — which made the wait considerably easier to justify.

All of this is history now: Big Walk unlocked everywhere across August 4 and 5, 2026, and no region is still waiting. The schedule above matters today mainly for context, and for anyone reconstructing exactly when their region first got in.

## Why Release Time Mattered For A Co-op Game

Big Walk is co-op only — you need at least one friend online to play at all, and sessions scale up to 12 players. That made the release time a *coordination* problem, not just a personal one. Groups split between the EU and US PS5 stores could not all start at the same moment; someone had to wait five hours for the others.

The cleanest solution was to **align on the latest unlock** in your group and start then, or have everyone use Steam, which unlocked globally together. Crossplay meant platform mixing was fine — a PS5 player and a Steam player could join the same session once both had access.

The same logic still applies to groups organising a first session today: pick one start time everyone can make across time zones, decide who hosts (the host owns the save file), and walk together. The launch stagger is gone, but time zones are forever.
