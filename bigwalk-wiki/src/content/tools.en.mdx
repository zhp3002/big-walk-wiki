Big Walk is a game about walking and talking, and its **tools and items** are what turn a group of lost friends into a navigating, signaling, puzzle-cracking team. By default **each player can hold one item at a time** (per games.gg's tools guide), so what you pick up — and who holds it — genuinely matters. This page covers the tools with verified information only: where to find the map, compass, GPS, walkie-talkies and binoculars, how each gadget works, and what your group should grab first.

![Four characters carrying binoculars and a walkie-talkie on a coastal cliff overlooking the ocean](/images/topics/cliff-items.jpg)

The official Steam page sums the category up as **"Tools and Toys: Walkie-talkies, laser pointers, binoculars, megaphones, flares, whiteboards (?), cowbells (??), and a big golden head (???)"** — the question marks are House House's own. The guide below fills in where the important ones actually live.

## How Many Items Can You Carry in Big Walk?

The carry rules, confirmed across TheGamer's and games.gg's tool guides:

- **Default: one item per player.** Whatever you hold occupies your hands.
- **Waist Bag: +1 small-item slot.** Found in the round blue buildings around the island; it only fits smaller items like the foldable map, radios and walkie-talkies.
- **Backpack: +1 full slot.** Also from the round blue buildings; it accepts any item, and a friend has to help you put it on.
- **Net result: a fully kitted player carries up to three items** — one in hand, one on the belt, one on the back. This matches the carry system described in our [all puzzles guide](/en/topics/all-puzzles).

## Navigation Tools

### Map — How do you get the map in Big Walk?

The foldable map sits in the **Map Room**, on a table (GameRant's walkthrough calls it a bench), next to the compass. The catch: the Map Room only opens after your group completes the [Red Tower](/en/topics/red-tower-puzzle) — five nearby puzzles, five puzzle rewards deposited, one filed key. The map you carry out is the handheld version of the room's giant floor diorama, and it shows the island's **numbered coordinate grid**, which is exactly what the [4166 1899 puzzle](/en/topics/4166-1899-puzzle) is built around.

### Compass

On the same table/bench in the Map Room (TheGamer and GameRant agree on the location). games.gg describes its function plainly: it shows **cardinal directions**, so you can orient the foldable map in the field instead of guessing which way the grid is rotated.

### Binoculars

![First-person view through binoculars of characters gathered in a golden grassy field](/images/topics/binoculars-view.jpg)

TheGamer places binoculars at **every control-panel tower** — the towers where puzzle rewards get deposited. Pick a pair up on your first tower visit. games.gg adds the use case: **zoom in to scout distant puzzle locations** before your group commits to a walk.

### GPS — where is it, and how does it work?

PC Gamer's walkthrough of the 4166 1899 puzzle describes a **GPS device in a red frame just outside the green tower**, next to a sign printed with more numbers. It displays the **live coordinates of whoever is holding it**: you can hold it up to read the screen, or **stick it onto a backpack** so it travels hands-free. The intended two-player play is one player wearing the GPS while another reads the screen and directs them — walk until the numbers match your target.

**Source note:** this device is described, in the coverage we checked in full, by PC Gamer's article only. Other tool guides (TheGamer, games.gg) do not list a separate GPS item, so treat these details as single-source for now. For the puzzle it solves, see the [4166 1899 solution](/en/topics/4166-1899-puzzle); the map route there needs no GPS at all.

## Communication Tools

### Walkie-Talkie — how do walkie-talkies work?

Big Walk's proximity voice chat fades with distance, echoes in tunnels and muffles through walls — walkie-talkies are the counter to all of it. With a **pair** of them, two players can talk from **any distance** (TheGamer, games.gg), and the official Steam page says the signal comes through **crackly**, which is part of the charm. TheGamer lists two findable spots: the **platform below the Red Tower** and the **red tunnel below the Red Tower**, and notes the unit can be mounted on your belt so your hands stay free — or stashed in a waist bag.

### Megaphone

On Steam's official tool list. TheGamer gives two locations: among the **yellow shapes north of the Yellow Tower** and in the **woods east of the Green Tower**. It amplifies your voice so the whole group can hear one caller.

### Radio

Found past the flashlight spot on the **red bridge** (TheGamer). It plays music — the practical use is leaving it somewhere as an **audio breadcrumb** the group can follow back.

### Laser Pointer

On the official list, with locations from TheGamer: the **Map Room table** and the **tops of towers**. games.gg's framing: point at the world to give nearby teammates a **precise direction** — much clearer than describing "the rock kind of left of the tree."

### Flare Gun

TheGamer: found **near most puzzle solution areas**, and the light **shoots upward**. games.gg adds what it is for: firing a flare **marks your approximate position on the map for the rest of the group** — the "we found something, come here" button.

### Whiteboard

On a **wall in the Map Room** (TheGamer) and on the official Steam list. Leave **written messages** for the group — useful for a plan everyone can re-read.

### Paintbrush

games.gg lists a paintbrush for **drawing markings on the ground and painting surfaces** so teammates can follow your trail. It does not appear in the other tool guides we read, so treat it as single-source.

## Light and Vision Tools

### Flashlight (Ball Lamp)

TheGamer places a flashlight **to the left of the red bridge from the starting area**, with more at the **tops of most colored towers**. games.gg covers the same need under the name **Ball Lamp** — a round lamp that lights your path at night, which their guide treats as mandatory for night navigation. Two names, same job: the guides agree the island gets dark.

### Telescopes

**Fixed telescopes** stand at lookout points — the mountain overlook in the [Red Tower](/en/topics/red-tower-puzzle) walkthrough is one example (TheGamer/GameRant). games.gg also lists a telescope tool that works like binoculars but **lights up distant targets and puzzle items**.

## Keys and Officially-Teased Oddities

- **Key Cutter** — games.gg names a key-cutting tool that **shapes raw keys into usable forms for the Tower Challenges**; this is the same key-filing step our Red Tower walkthrough covers at the contraptions between the tower and the gym. Single-source naming (games.gg), but the filing step itself is confirmed by GameRant.
- **Cowbells** — on the official Steam list with House House's own "??". No location in any guide we read.
- **The big golden head** — officially listed with "???" and described in official materials as the blindfold-style prop for an obstacle-course activity.

## What Tools Should You Get First?

There is no required order, but the coverage points at a sensible opening shopping list:

1. **Map and compass** — finish the [Red Tower](/en/topics/red-tower-puzzle) early. Every later puzzle hunt gets easier once the island has coordinates.
2. **Two walkie-talkies** — grab the pair around the Red Tower platform area and stay in contact when the group splits.
3. **Binoculars** — pick them up at the first control-panel tower you climb.
4. **A backpack or waist bag** — check the round blue buildings; +1 or +2 carry slots changes how much gadgetry the group can field at once.

## FAQ

### How do you get the map in Big Walk?

Complete the [Red Tower](/en/topics/red-tower-puzzle) — five nearby puzzles, five puzzle rewards deposited at the top terminal, and the filed key — to unlock the Map Room. The foldable map is on a table inside, next to the compass.

### Where is the GPS in Big Walk?

In a **red frame just outside the green tower**, next to a numbered sign, per PC Gamer's walkthrough. This is the only source in our checked coverage that describes the GPS as its own item.

### How does the GPS work in Big Walk?

It shows the **live coordinates of its holder** on a screen. Hold it up to read, or stick it on a backpack: one player wears it and walks while another reads the numbers and directs them — the standard method for the 4166 1899 coordinates puzzle.

### How do walkie-talkies work in Big Walk?

Pick up **two** of them (platform and red tunnel below the Red Tower) and the holders can talk at **any distance**, with a crackly signal. Belt-mounting or waist-bag storage keeps hands free.

### What tools should you get first in Big Walk?

Map and compass via the Red Tower, then a pair of walkie-talkies, binoculars from the first tower you climb, and a backpack or waist bag from a round blue building.

### How many items can you carry in Big Walk?

One by default. A waist bag adds a small-items slot and a backpack adds a full slot — up to **three** items for a fully kitted player.

### Where do you get a backpack in Big Walk?

In the **round blue buildings** around the island (TheGamer; also covered in our [all puzzles guide](/en/topics/all-puzzles)). A friend has to help you equip it.

### Is there a flashlight in Big Walk?

Yes — to the left of the red bridge from the starting area, and at the tops of most colored towers (TheGamer). games.gg calls the same night-lighting item the Ball Lamp.
